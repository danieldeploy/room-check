### Reviewing a TOConline batch

Each original submission retains its attachment identities and its sending account.
First apply the authenticated, individually identified outcomes. For purchase rows
without an e-Fatura number, compare the issuer counts with the recorded email. A
complete, unique supplier group with only successful outcomes can be marked as
confirmed by the batch. The Hub does not assign individual purchase references
from a count. Duplicate messages do not add results. An unknown supplier, an
incomplete response or two equally plausible sends cannot establish acceptance.
The signed reason for a refusal is kept at the level the reply identifies.

A batch requiring intervention appears under Problems or pending. Each invoice
has its own PDF preview and status. A reviewer can compare all members, including
confirmed documents, against TOConline. A manager identifies an unnamed refusal
separately from a missing result. A missing result alone never authorizes a new
email. The explicit resend checks late replies, eligibility and the shared mail
allowance before reserving one PDF. The prior attempt remains in the ledger.
A manually identified duplicate does not authorize automatic deletion of the PDF.
Other documents and months continue. The existing consolidated manager summary
reports unresolved exceptions with their known cause and action.

Retained replies go through the normal signature, recipient and timestamp checks.
The release and worker can reconcile old submissions using that evidence without
resending the original PDFs. An unresolved outcome remains visible when retained
mail does not prove what happened.
